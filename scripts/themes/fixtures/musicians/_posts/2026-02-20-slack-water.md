Slack Water is the twenty minutes between tides when nothing moves. These four
tracks were built from recordings made in that window, once a month for a year.

## Tracklist

1. Second Mooring Post (5:12)
2. Bait Shop Radio (4:03)
3. Fenders (6:40)
4. Slack Water (8:21)

Mastered at the boathouse. Tape edition of 100, download everywhere else.
