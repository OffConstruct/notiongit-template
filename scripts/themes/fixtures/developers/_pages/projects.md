## Tidewatch

An open-source library that predicts the tide for any harbour with survey
data. Used by about forty harbour offices. [Read the docs](https://docs.example.net/tidewatch/).

## Mooring map

A small web app that shows free visitor moorings in real time.

## Tide tables dataset

Hand-copied tide observations from 1890 to 1910, cleaned and published.
