Tidewatch 2.0 predicts a full year of tides in under a second, about ten
times faster than 1.x. Setup is now one command, and the new guide walks a
harbour office through its first table.

## What changed

- A new prediction engine, rewritten in Rust.
- One install command for every platform.
- Clear error messages when survey data is missing.

Thank you to the forty contributors who made this release.
