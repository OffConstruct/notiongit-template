A commonplace book is a notebook of things worth keeping. Mine is a
sketchbook with an index.[^1] Here is how it works.

## The rules

1. Write the date before anything else.
2. Copy the source with every note, even a page number.
3. Never delete an entry; cross it out instead.

**Paper** - a plain A5 book with numbered pages.

**Index** - the last four pages, by subject.

## A worked example

| Subject | Pages | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Boats | 4, 17, 52 | pencil and wash |
| Hands | 9, 10 | from life |
| Skies | 33 | color notes |

> Draw what you see, not what you know.

When the notes moved to a computer I wrote a tiny script to rebuild the index:

```text
boats   4 17 52
hands   9 10
skies   33
```

[^1]: The first one is from art school and mostly drawings of chairs.
